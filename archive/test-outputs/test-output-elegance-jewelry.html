Ring Size Guide

How to Measure Your Ring Size

Use a piece of string or paper and wrap it around your finger. Mark the point where it overlaps, then measure the length in millimeters.

US Size UK Size EU Size Circumference (mm)
5 J 49 49.3
6 L 51 51.9
7 N 54 54.4
8 P 57 57.0
9 R 59 59.5
