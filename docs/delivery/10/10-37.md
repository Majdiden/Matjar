# [10-37] No made-up store claims in themes

[Back to task list](./tasks.md)

## Description

Product pages and other storefront parts showed theme demo claims the store never made: "Free shipping on orders over $50", "Estimated delivery 3–5 business days", "30-day returns", "100% authentic", "Secure SSL checkout", invented testimonials and press logos, free-shipping progress bars with a fixed amount (checkout has no free-shipping threshold), five stars on products without reviews.

- `useTrustLines()` (shared, `components/commerce/TrustBadges.tsx`) gives the store's real facts from the merchant's delivery, returns and payment answers, in the shopper's language. Every theme's product page shows them in its own style, or nothing.
- Shipping and returns tabs show the store's published policies.
- Trust, benefit, feature, testimonial, press, ticker, promo and banner sections show only what the merchant typed (`useMerchantBlocks`, `merchantText`), else the trust lines where that fits, else nothing. Manifest demo blocks were emptied; old demo copies already saved in stores are recognised and hidden.
- Free-shipping bars removed; star ratings only with reviews; contact page shows only the store's own email, phone, address and hours.
