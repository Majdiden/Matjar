# [10-45] Store logo clear in every header, at a size that fits its shape

[Back to task list](./tasks.md)

## Description

A merchant uploaded a phone screenshot of their logo: black bars and a status bar around a small square. Every header gave it a fixed height, so it showed as a thin dark strip.

- **Crop at upload** (`services/logoTrim.js`, used by `POST /api/upload/logo`): a transparent, near-white or near-black margin is cut away, and a thin strip of marks at the very top or bottom (a status bar) is dropped. A logo on a coloured tile keeps its tile, and nothing is cropped when the margin is small. The result is stored as PNG, so transparency is kept (local uploads no longer turn it black).
- **Existing logos**: `node scripts/trim-store-logos.js --dry-run`, then without `--dry-run`, crops the logos uploaded before this (a new image; the old one stays).
- **Size by shape** (`_shared/components/StoreLogo.tsx`, `_shared/theme/logoScale.ts`): the slot keeps the theme's height; tall logos are drawn up to 20% taller, wide logos shorter and wider within the theme's max width. Used in all 18 theme headers and the shared phone menu.
