# [10-12] "متجري" (My store) hub + brand kit form

[Back to task list](./tasks.md)

## Description

A phone-first "My store" hub at `/dashboard/store` and the brand-kit form ("Store info", هوية المتجر) at `/dashboard/store/brand`, both behind `design.simpleMode`. The hub is the simple way into everything a customer sees; the form edits the brand kit through `GET/PUT /api/store-profile` (10-3/10-4) one field at a time, with autosave that survives dropped connections.

## Requirements

1. Everything is behind `design.simpleMode`; with the flag off the nav entries are hidden and the routes show the existing "not available" card. Nothing else changes.
2. Hub (`/dashboard/store`): a store card (logo, name, store address, "View my store", Share via `ShareButton`), then one big card per destination with an icon, a title and a one-line status:
   - Store info → `/dashboard/store/brand` — "Done" or the first missing fact ("Add your logo", "Add your WhatsApp number", …).
   - Homepage → `/dashboard/store/homepage` (10-13).
   - About us → `/dashboard/store/about`, Contact us → `/dashboard/store/contact`, Delivery & returns → `/dashboard/store/policies` (routes registered by 10-9..10-11). Contact shows "Ready" once WhatsApp is set, else "Add your WhatsApp number first".
   - Change the look → `/dashboard/themes`.
   - Advanced options → `/dashboard/themes/editor`, marked "best on a computer".
   Cards are filtered by the staff member's permissions.
3. Navigation: "My store" in the sidebar (Storefront group) and in the phone bottom nav, both gated by the flag.
4. Brand form, one fact per card: logo (upload), store name, slogan (Arabic, optional English — 10-14), cover photo (upload), main colour (8 swatches + the phone's own colour picker + "use the look's own colour"), WhatsApp (pre-filled from the account phone with a "Yes, use this number" button; "Customers will message you on this number"), city, opening hours, Facebook / Instagram / TikTok.
5. Autosave per field on blur / tap with a small "Saving… / Saved" indicator. Edits are kept on the phone until the server has them; network and server failures retry with backoff and on `online`; a rejected value is shown with a friendly localized message and kept in the field.
6. Photos are shrunk on the phone before upload; a failed upload keeps the photo and retries when the connection is back.
7. Live phone-sized storefront preview: beside the form on desktop, behind a "Preview" button on phones; refreshed after each save.
8. Works at 360–430px in RTL and LTR with no horizontal scroll; tap targets ≥ 44px; no design vocabulary; Arabic copy per the PRD copy guideline.

## Implementation Plan

- `dashboard/src/lib/storeProfile.ts`: profile/patch types mirroring the API, limits mirrored from `utils/brandKit.js`, colour presets, `firstMissingBrandItem` (hub status), `socialInputToLink` ("@name" → profile link), `unwrapWhatsappLink` (same rule as the backend), `profileErrorMessage` (server 400s → friendly per-field copy on top of `lib/api-errors.ts`).
- `dashboard/src/lib/api-client.ts`: `api.storeProfile.get/update`.
- `dashboard/src/hooks/useStoreProfile.ts`: `useStoreProfile` (per-store localStorage cache, so the screens open offline) and `useProfileAutosave` (per-field queue: draft persisted before sending, coalescing of edits made while a save is in flight, retry with 3/6/12/24/30s backoff + `online`, no retry on 4xx, `retry` for "try now").
- `dashboard/src/hooks/useFieldValue.ts`: field value that follows the server until edited.
- `dashboard/src/lib/shrinkImage.ts`: ≤1600px WebP re-encode when it saves bytes.
- `dashboard/src/pages/store/StoreHub.tsx`, `BrandKit.tsx`, `brandFields.tsx` (FieldCard, SaveIndicator, ImageField, ColorField), `PhonePreview.tsx` (390px storefront scaled into a phone frame, owner preview URL from `GET /store-setup/starter`; the theme editor's `PreviewFrame` is a desktop browser-chrome canvas, so a small dedicated frame was simpler than bending it).
- `dashboard/src/pages/store/HomepagePlaceholder.tsx`: **placeholder** for `/dashboard/store/homepage`, clearly marked for 10-13 to replace (file, route and `homepage_placeholder.*` strings).
- `App.tsx` routes; `components/layouts/nav.ts` + `BottomNav.tsx` entries with `feature: 'design.simpleMode'`.
- i18n: new `storeDesign` namespace (en + ar) registered in `i18n/resources.ts`; nav labels in `nav.json` (`sidebar.storefront.my_store`, `bottom_nav.store`).
- No backend changes.
