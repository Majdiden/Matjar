## Requirements

1. Behind `onboarding.v2`, evaluated per store (the dashboard's tenant-resolved flags), so the operator can switch it on for the test store only.
2. Three steps, progress "1 of 3" with a bar. The first undone step is highlighted and its button is the primary one.
3. Completion rules (pure, in `dashboard/src/lib/onboarding.ts` `firstSaleProgress`):
   - **Add your first product**: done when the store has ≥ 1 product. The button opens `/dashboard/products/new`.
   - **How customers pay**: done when customers have a way to pay **and** the merchant has looked at it. That means either they reviewed the step (`tenant.onboarding.paymentsReviewedAt` is set), or they switched on a method other than cash on delivery (e.g. Bankak or other manual transfer). With every method off it is never done. If the methods can't be read (`payments.methods` off, so COD is the only method and always on), reviewing is enough.
     - `payments.methods` on: the button "Choose how you get paid" opens `/dashboard/payments/methods` and stamps the review.
     - Off: the step says cash on delivery is on, and the button "OK, that works for me" stamps the review.
   - **Share your store**: done once the merchant has shared from the checklist (`tenant.onboarding.sharedAt`). The button is the general ShareButton with the store URL and a ready message. "Shared" means the native share sheet completed, an app was picked in the fallback menu, or the link was copied. Dismissing the sheet does not count.
4. Small API for what can't be inferred: `GET /api/onboarding` → `{ flow, sharedAt, paymentsReviewedAt }` and `POST /api/onboarding/events { event: "shared" | "payments_reviewed" }`. Only the FIRST time is stored (idempotent), and the call is tenant-scoped and needs `settings.read` or `settings.write`. The timestamps double as time-to-share for 10-18.
5. "Make it yours" row (logo, colour, cover photo) links to `/dashboard/store/brand` (built by 10-12). It is shown only when `design.simpleMode` is also on, so the link never leads to a hidden page.
6. Dismissible: an × hides the card (remembered per browser). The card also disappears once all three steps are done. Under v2 the quick-actions card shows its static links instead of the old setup steps.
7. Phone-first: 48px full-width buttons on phones, no horizontal scroll at 360–430px in RTL and LTR.
