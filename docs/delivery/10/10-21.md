# [10-21] Quick add product (first sale, step 1)

[Back to task list](./tasks.md)

## Description

The PBI 10 overview promised that the first checklist step would be "camera, name, price". Instead, "Add your first product" opened the full product form, which requires a description of at least 10 characters, a SKU, a category and stock, and offers variants, SEO and English fields. That is too much for a first-time seller. This task adds a phone-first quick form with photos, name, price and quantity, plus an optional description. The product is published at once and can be shared straight away.

## Requirements

1. `POST /api/products/quick` accepts only `name` (1–200 characters, trimmed), `price` (> 0), `stock` (whole number, 0 to 1,000,000), `images` (up to 10 http(s) links) and an optional `description` (≤ 5000 characters). Unknown keys are rejected with 400. It uses the same auth, `products.write` permission and plan limit as `POST /api/products`.
2. The server fills in the rest:
   - The product is published (`active`).
   - The description is the name when none is given.
   - The SKU is generated as `QA-` + 10 hex digits. The `(tenantId, sku)` index treats a missing SKU as a value, so every product needs its own.
   - The link (slug) is derived from the name, as in 10-20.
   - The category is the store's "منتجاتنا / Our products" category.
3. That category is created on first use and found again by `systemKey: "quick-add"`, so renaming it or changing its link never creates a second one. A unique partial index on `(tenantId, systemKey)` makes concurrent first products share one category. Clients can't set `systemKey` through the category API.
4. Dashboard page `/dashboard/products/quick`:
   - Photos come first (camera or gallery). Then name, price with the store currency, and quantity with − / + buttons, starting at 1. Zero shows "Out of stock" on the storefront, which the help text explains.
   - The description sits behind "Add a description (optional)".
   - Prices and quantities typed with Arabic digits, the Arabic decimal mark or thousands separators are understood.
   - After saving, a success screen shows the product with Share (native share sheet or fallback menu), Add another, Back to home and Edit details.
   - A link opens the full form.
5. Where it's used:
   - The "first sale" checklist (`onboarding.v2`) always opens the quick form.
   - With `design.simpleMode`, every "Add product" button opens it: the bottom-bar quick action, the home header, the old setup checklist, the home quick actions, and the Products page header and empty state. Otherwise those buttons keep the full form.
6. Arabic copy is plain, natural standard Arabic.

## Implementation Plan

- Backend:
  - `validators/product.validator.js`: `QUICK_PRODUCT_LIMITS` and `createQuickProductSchema` (strict, web-only image links).
  - `services/product.js`: `createProduct` is shared by `addProduct` and `addQuickProduct`.
  - `services/category.js`: `ensureQuickAddCategory`, plus stripping `systemKey` on category create and update.
  - `schemas/store/category.js`: `systemKey` and its partial unique index.
  - `controllers/product.js`: `createQuickProductController`, wrapped in `asyncHandler`.
  - `routes/product.js`: `POST /quick`.
- Dashboard:
  - `lib/quickProduct.ts`: limits, routes, `parsePriceInput`, `parseQuantityInput`, `stepQuantity`, `productLink`.
  - `hooks/useNewProductRoute.ts`.
  - `pages/products/QuickProduct.tsx`.
  - `App.tsx` route; `api.products.createQuick`.
  - `FirstSaleChecklist.tsx`, `BottomNav.tsx`, `Dashboard.tsx`, `Products.tsx`.
  - i18n `products.quick.*` (en and ar).

## Test Plan

- E2E `tests/e2e/quick-product.test.js`:
  - Published at once, with a readable link, a generated SKU, the name as description, and the Arabic category.
  - The description is kept, and the category is reused after the merchant renames it.
  - 13 bad bodies (empty or blank name, zero, negative or string price, fractional, negative or missing quantity, `javascript:` image, extra `status`, `category` or `tenantId`) give 400 and write nothing.
  - 401 without a token.
  - Three concurrent first products share one category.
- Unit `tests/unit/quickProduct.test.js`: Arabic digits, decimal mark and separators; rejected inputs; quantity steps and bounds; the product link; dashboard and server limits match.
- Headless Chromium at 360 and 390 px, Arabic and English:
  - No horizontal overflow.
  - Error state.
  - Filled form sends `{"price":15000,"stock":3,…}` for "١٥٬٠٠٠" and two taps on +.
  - Success screen.
  - The home page links to the quick form under simple mode.
