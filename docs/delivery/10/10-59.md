# [10-59] Regional pricing: own prices per currency, rounding, rate safeguards, payment methods per region, automatic rates

[Back to task list](./tasks.md)

## Description

Scenarios from the merchant: "Sudan is priced in USD and converted, but Saudi Arabia should be real SAR prices". Each item below follows from one of those scenarios.

**Own prices per currency (fallback: converted)**
- Products and variants have `prices` ({ SAR: 99 }). The product form has "Your own prices in other currencies": one box per rated currency, with the converted price as the placeholder. A variant with its own price has its own boxes.
- `services/regionalPricing.js` decides the unit price in the shopper's region. It uses the merchant's own price for that currency, else price × adjustment × rate, rounded by the region's rule.
- **Checkout:** `priceCheckout` prices such lines at the pricing-currency equivalent of what is paid, so discounts, tax and the presentment conversion land on the exact amount. The conversion uses unrounded amounts.
- **Orders:** orders keep `products[].presentmentPrice` and `presentmentDiscount`.
- **Storefront:** `middlewares/regionalPrices.js` rewrites product responses (products, categories, collections, wishlist) and the cart response the same way.
  - The storefront sends `X-Matjar-Market` / `X-Matjar-Currency`; without them, the visitor's detected region is used.
  - `Vary` is set, and CORS allows both headers.
  - Changing region or currency reloads the page so prices are fetched again.
- Order pages and the account list show line prices and totals as paid (`formatOrderLine`, `formatOrderAmount`). The tracking API returns the presentment totals.

**Rounding**
- Per region (`markets[].rounding`) and for the website currency (`currencies.rounding`).
- Options: none, whole number, up to the next 5/10/50/100/500/1,000, or ending in .99. Converted prices are rounded up; own prices are never rounded.

**Safeguards**
- A region's currency must be the pricing currency or have a rate. Creating or editing one without a rate is refused (400, `MARKET_CURRENCY_NO_RATE`), and the dashboard blocks it too.
- An existing region whose rate was removed gets a "No exchange rate" badge. It shows prices in the pricing currency with its adjustment, instead of being dropped from the storefront or losing its adjustment at checkout.

**Payment methods per region**
- `markets[].paymentMethods` lists the codes a region offers; empty means all. They are chosen in the region form.
- `/storefront/payment-methods?market=` filters by region. Checkout asks with the quoted region and clears a method that is no longer offered.
- Placing an order with a method its region doesn't offer is refused.

**Automatic exchange rates**
- In Currencies, each rate row can "Update automatically" with a margin.
- The rate is the platform's exchange-rate table (Platform → Billing → FX) plus the margin. It is written into `rates` on save, and for every such store whenever the platform updates its table (`services/autoRates.js`).
- A currency the platform table doesn't cover is refused (400, `AUTO_RATE_UNAVAILABLE`), and the merchant types that rate (e.g. SDG).
