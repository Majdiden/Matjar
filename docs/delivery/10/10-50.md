# [10-50] Delivery areas at checkout from the merchant's shipping zones

[Back to task list](./tasks.md)

## Description

Shipping zones set in Settings → Shipping were never used at checkout. Zones were matched by country only, so in a store whose zones are all in one country every order was charged the first zone's price.

- When the store delivers by zone, checkout replaces the area field with a «منطقة التوصيل» list of the merchant's zone names. There is no free-text option.
- The quote prices the picked zone (by id). Its price shows on the shipping line of the order summary, and the total includes it.
- The store payload (`deliveryZones`) carries zone ids and names only. Prices stay on the server.
- Placing an order with several zones and no pick (or an unknown or deleted zone) is refused with `DELIVERY_ZONE_REQUIRED`. Checkout then returns to the address step and asks for the area. A store with one zone uses it without asking.
- The order records the zone: `shippingAddress.state` is the zone's name and `shippingAddress.deliveryZoneId` is its id, whatever area text was sent.
- `/api/orders` accepts `shippingAddress.deliveryZoneId`.
