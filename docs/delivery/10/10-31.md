# [10-31] Generated policies in both languages

[Back to task list](./tasks.md)

## Description

Returns, delivery and cash on delivery policies were written only in the store's language, so Arabic shoppers of an English store (and the other way round) read the wrong language. The generator now writes both languages into `settings.policies.<key>.translations`; the storefront shows the shopper's language on the policy page, the footer, checkout and product pages. A policy the merchant edited by hand has no translations sent to the storefront, so their own words show in every language.
