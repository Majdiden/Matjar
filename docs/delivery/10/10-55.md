# [10-55] Announcement emails: brand banner design with the Matjar logo

[Back to task list](./tasks.md)

## Description

Announcement emails use the "brand banner" design (the middle one of the three previewed), with the user's changes: no store logo, the Matjar logo on a white tile so it stands out from the blue band, and the footer logo stacked above the name.

- **Band:** a blue Matjar band with the logo on a white tile, the name "Matjar", and «رسالة من فريق Matjar» / "A message from the Matjar team".
- **Message card:** a white card with the title, the paragraphs and a rounded button, all centred.
- **Footer:** the Matjar logo above its name, which store the person runs, the notification-settings link, and the open-tracking image.

The layout uses tables and inline styles, with `bgcolor` as the band's fallback colour. The Matjar logo is `/dashboard/pwa-192x192.png` on the app host.
