# [10-55] Announcement emails: "store first" design with logos

[Back to task list](./tasks.md)

## Description

Announcement emails use the "store first" design (option C of the three previewed):
- **Header:** the store's logo (88px, rounded) and name, with a «رسالة من فريق Matjar» / "A message from the Matjar team" label. A store without a logo gets a tile with its first letter in its brand colour (Matjar blue when none is set).
- **Message card:** a white card with a blue top line holding the title, the paragraphs and a full-width button that is easy to tap on phones.
- **Footer:** the Matjar icon and name, why the person got the email, the notification-settings link, and the open-tracking image.

The layout uses tables and inline styles only, for Gmail, Outlook and phone mail apps.

**Where the images come from**
- The store logo comes from `settings.logo` when it is an https URL, which is what production uploads are. A local `/uploads` path (development) is made absolute.
- The Matjar icon is `/dashboard/pwa-192x192.png` on the app host.
