# [10-55] Announcement emails: new design with the Matjar logo

[Back to task list](./tasks.md)

## Description

Announcement emails use the "store first" layout (option C of the three previewed). On request, the header shows only the Matjar logo, not the store's:
- **Header:** the Matjar logo (64px) and name, with a «رسالة من فريق Matjar» / "A message from the Matjar team" label.
- **Message card:** a white card with a blue top line holding the title, the paragraphs and a full-width button that is easy to tap on phones.
- **Footer:** which store the person runs, the notification-settings link, and the open-tracking image.

The layout uses tables and inline styles only, for Gmail, Outlook and phone mail apps. The Matjar logo is `/dashboard/pwa-192x192.png` on the app host.
