# [10-55] Announcement emails: clean letter design with the Matjar logo

[Back to task list](./tasks.md)

## Description

Announcement emails use the "clean letter" design (the left one of the three previewed), with the user's changes: no store logo, and the footer logo stacked above the name.

- **Card header:** a white card on a light grey page. The header row has the Matjar logo, the name "Matjar" and «رسالة من فريق Matjar» / "A message from the Matjar team".
- **Message:** below a thin line, the title, the paragraphs and a blue button, aligned with the reading direction.
- **Footer:** the Matjar logo above its name, which store the person runs, the notification-settings link, and the open-tracking image.

**Preview line:** a hidden line at the top of the email holds the start of the message, followed by invisible filler. Without it, Gmail and iPhone notifications and inbox lists showed "Matjar · رسالة من فريق Matjar · <the title again>" before the message.

The layout uses tables and inline styles only. The Matjar logo is `/dashboard/pwa-192x192.png` on the app host.
