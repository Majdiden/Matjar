# [10-32] Theme footers and heroes show only the store's content

[Back to task list](./tasks.md)

## Description

Footers mixed English links, theme demo links (best sellers, ingredients, careers...) and fake social icons; heroes showed theme demo eyebrows, second buttons and review lines; slideshows and collages kept the theme's stock photos next to the merchant's.

- Every theme's footer is built from `useStoreFooter()` (shared): store name or logo, description, Shop (all products and up to 5 categories), Help (about, contact), Policies (published ones, in the shopper's language), social links and contact lines only when set, and a translated copyright line.
- Hero extras (eyebrow, second button, badges) show only when the merchant set them (`merchantText`); stock slides after the first are dropped once the merchant has a photo, and collage images fall back to the merchant's photo or product photos (`merchantImage`, `isStockImage`).
- The WhatsApp button on the Contact page fits its text.
- New arrivals / featured products no longer store English headings; the storefront shows its translated heading.
