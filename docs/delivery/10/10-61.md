# [10-61] Markets as the one place for currencies and prices

[Back to task list](./tasks.md)

## Description

Two tabs (Currencies, Markets), a website currency and a visitor currency switcher made pricing hard to follow. Merchants now only add markets, and each card shows exactly what its customers pay.

**Settings → Markets & currencies (one tab)**
- **Pricing currency**: "You write product prices in US Dollar (USD)", with a change button and a note that the price numbers stay the same.
- **Market cards** show:
  - flags, countries, and the currency name and code;
  - how prices are set, e.g. "Converted from your price at 1 USD = 2,600 SDG · up to the next 100 · updated automatically", or "Your own prices for 12 of 15 products, the rest converted at …";
  - a real product as customers see it ("Rose perfume: $20 → customers see 52,000 SDG");
  - payment methods, and badges for default, off and no exchange rate.
- **Add / edit a market** is one screen:
  1. Countries by flag. The name, currency and rounding fill in from the first country.
  2. Prices: "Convert my prices" or "Write fixed prices", the exchange rate (⇄ to type it either way, or "update automatically" plus a margin when the platform has the currency), and rounding.
  3. Payment methods.
  - A live preview shows three real products and the conversion formula.
  - More options: price adjustment, language, default market, on/off.
- **Fixed prices table**: every product with its converted price as the hint. Only changed rows are saved, via `PUT /products/:id { prices }`.
- The product form's own prices are labelled by market ("Price in Saudi Arabia (SAR)").
- `?tab=currencies` opens Markets. The Markets tab and its APIs open with either the `settings.markets` or the `settings.currencies` switch (`requireAnyFeature`).

**Server**
- Markets have `pricing` (convert / fixed).
- A market's `rate` or `autoRate: { margin }` is saved with it, into `currencies.rates` / `currencies.auto` (`marketRateUpdate`).
- The first market becomes the default; deleting the default market makes the next one the default.

**Storefront**
- The free visitor currency switcher is gone; it could only show approximate prices. Customers choose a market (header menu and phone menu), or are matched to one by country.

**Migration 019**
- A store's website currency becomes a default market (no countries, same currency and rounding), and `display` / `showSwitcher` are cleared.
- The previous values are kept for `down()`.
