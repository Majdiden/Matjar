## Requirements

- Phones: the preview is full width (no phone frame), pinned at the top of the page (38% of the screen), above the list and any open sheet. Desktop keeps the phone frame beside the list.
- Tap to edit: the editor sends `PICK_MODE`; the storefront ThemeProvider swallows taps inside `[data-section-id]` and replies `SECTION_PICKED`; the part being edited is outlined (`HIGHLIGHT_SECTION`). Only inside the editor iframe, behind the existing parent/origin checks.
- Text fields send each keystroke to the preview without saving; saving happens when the field is left.
- 'Saved' floats above the bottom bar and fades after 3.5 s; saving, retrying and errors stay until resolved.
- Top strip row (first, fixed): show/hide and Arabic-first text, saved with a new `theme` op (PUT theme settings), with undo and offline retry.
- Product grids are named by what they show (newest / featured); every theme's hero and button field have plain names.
- A section may have 4 basic settings (heading, text, photo, button).

## Test Plan

Unit: `tests/unit/homepageEditor.test.js` (theme ops: keys, bilingual flat values, apply/undo), `themeBindings` (4 basic allowed, 5 rejected). Headless Chromium at 390px with a mocked preview: tapping the hero and the strip opens their sheets; typed text appears in the preview before any save; 'Saved' disappears after ~4 s; no horizontal overflow.
