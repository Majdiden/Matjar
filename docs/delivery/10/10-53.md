# [10-53] Email links open on the app host (app.matjar.to)

[Back to task list](./tasks.md)

## Description

Links in emails opened `matjar.to/...` (from a dashboard URL env var set to the bare domain) or the store's own address (staff invitations, staff password resets). They now all open on the app host, `https://app.matjar.to`:

- notification and announcement emails, the notification-settings footer link, and the beta signup link;
- staff invitations (`/dashboard/staff/accept`): the page finds the store from the token, so no login or store address is needed;
- staff password resets (`/dashboard/reset-password`). Customer resets stay on their store's address;
- platform console invitations and resets (`/platform/...`).

`config.appOrigin` is `https://app.<platform domain>` (http for local hosts). `config.appUrlOr()` keeps `PUBLIC_DASHBOARD_URL` / `DASHBOARD_URL` / `PLATFORM_ADMIN_URL` only when they point at the app host.
