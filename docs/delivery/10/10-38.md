## Description

Merchants did not understand "Domains / النطاقات". The menu entry, page and every merchant-facing mention now say "Store link / رابط المتجر", the word merchants already use when they share their store on WhatsApp. The free address is "Your free link / رابطك المجاني"; a domain the merchant bought is "your own link / رابط خاص", with "domain / دومين" named once in brackets where they connect one, since that is the word domain sellers use. DNS steps keep their technical names (merchants copy them to their domain seller). Platform admin (staff only) and shipping "weight ranges" (نطاقات وزن) are unchanged.
