# [10-62] Checkout address: the store's countries only, city lists, delivery area from the country

[Back to task list](./tasks.md)

## Description

Checkout asked for country, city and a separate delivery area, and offered every country.

**What changes**
- **Countries:** checkout lists only the countries the store delivers to (`store.shipCountries`, `services/shipping.js` `storeCountries`). These are the delivery zones' countries, else the markets' countries, else the platform's countries.
  - With one country, it is chosen for the shopper and only the city is asked.
- **Cities:** the selected country's cities are listed. Sudan already had a list; Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman, Egypt, Jordan, South Sudan and Turkey now do too, in Arabic and English. "مدينة أخرى" (other city) lets the shopper type a city that isn't listed.
- **Delivery area:** it comes from the country. The shopper picks one only when their country has several areas (e.g. Khartoum and Omdurman), and only from that country's areas.
  - The server does the same: `calculateShipping` takes the single area of the country, and refuses an area that belongs to another country.

**Fix: country names vs codes**
- Addresses carry the country's English name ("Saudi Arabia"), while markets, delivery zones and tax rates use ISO codes ("SA").
- As a result, the market was never matched by the delivery country (checkout always fell back to the default market), and tax rates for a country never applied.
- `utils/countryCode.js` `toCountryCode` now normalises the country in `resolveMarket`, `calculateShipping` and `calculateTax`.
