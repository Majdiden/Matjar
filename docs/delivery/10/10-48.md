# [10-48] Storefront on phones: clear product photos, readable store name and logo, working contact details

[Back to task list](./tasks.md)

## Description

Feedback from a new store (Misk theme) on a phone:

- **Contact page** (`_shared/pages/Contact.tsx`, used by every theme): every value the merchant typed keeps its own direction (`dir="auto"`), so opening hours like «9ص-6م» no longer come out scrambled in an English page. The phone has "Call now" (and the store's WhatsApp when set), the email is a mail link and the address opens the map. The automatic contact page gets the same direction fix.
- **Product cards** (Misk and Linen, the two themes whose add-to-cart bar covered the photo): on phones and touch screens the add button sits under the price, lined up across the row; the quick-view eye is left out; only a small heart stays on the photo. Desktop keeps the hover bar.
- **Store name in the header** (all 18 themes): on phones it may take two lines at a slightly smaller size instead of being cut with "…"; one line on desktop.
- **Logo**: the phone logo slot is 44px (was 32–40px) in every theme. Logos drawn on a coloured square (a gold mark on purple) are now cut down around the mark at upload, keeping a band of the square's colour, so the mark fills the header slot. Existing logos: run `node scripts/trim-store-logos.js`.
