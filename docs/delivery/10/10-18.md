# [10-18] Merchant validation rounds

[Back to task list](./tasks.md)

## Description

PBI 10 was designed from research and two real signups, not from watching merchants use the new flow. This task is the field test: short, observed sessions with real Sudanese merchants, run three times, with the product changed between rounds. It needs people, so it can't be completed in code. This file is the script.

## Who

- 3–5 merchants per round who sell today on Facebook/WhatsApp, mostly women selling from home, on their own Android phones.
- At least one who has never used a website builder.
- Not the two merchants who already registered (they have learned the old flow), except as a control.

## Setup per session (about 30 minutes)

1. Turn on `onboarding.v2` for the session (`?flow=v2` on the register link) and `design.simpleMode` for the test store afterwards (platform admin → store → feature overrides).
2. The merchant uses their own phone and network. Don't help, don't explain words. If they're stuck for over 60 seconds, note where, then give the smallest possible hint.
3. Record (with permission) the screen and their comments. Ask them to think aloud in their own words.

## Tasks to give (read them aloud, in Arabic)

1. "Open your own shop on Matjar." (signup: name, store name, link, what you sell, city, look)
2. "Add one product you sell, with a photo and a price."
3. "Make the shop show your logo and your colour."
4. "Put your WhatsApp number so customers can message you."
5. "Send your shop link to a friend on WhatsApp."
6. "Change the big picture at the top of your shop's front page."

## What to write down for each task

| Measure | How |
|---|---|
| Completed without help? | yes / with hint / failed |
| Time | stopwatch from reading the task to done |
| Where they hesitated | the screen and the field |
| Words they didn't understand | exact words, in Arabic |
| What they expected instead | their own words |
| Errors | anything typed into the wrong field (e.g. a Facebook link as the store link) |

## Product metrics (from production data, per round)

- % of new stores with at least one product within 24 hours of signup.
- Median time from signup to first share (tenant `onboarding.sharedAt`).
- % of stores with a first order within 7 days.
- % of stores that ever open the advanced editor.
- Signup drop-off per step (when funnel tracking exists).
