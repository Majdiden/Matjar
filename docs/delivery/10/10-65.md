# [10-65] Storefront: category titles on phones, no gap under the footer, generic search placeholder

[Back to task list](./tasks.md)

## Description

**Category title**
- In nine themes the title sat beside the sort list, so a long Arabic name ("النساء بمختلف الأعمار") wrapped word by word in a narrow column.
- On phones the title now takes its own line (24px) with the sort list full width below it. They sit side by side from `sm` up.
- Themes: artisan, bookshelf, elegance, freshmart, homedecor, kidsworld, modern, sportzone, starter.
- Aurum, glowing, beauxe, milmaa and nutreko had 48px titles on phones; these are now 30–36px on phones, unchanged from `sm`/`md` up.

**Gap under the footer**
- The room kept for the floating phone menu was body padding, which showed as a blank strip below the footer. Four themes (artisan, elegance, freshmart, modern) also added their own `pb-16` on top.
- A page ending in a footer now gets that room inside the footer (`footer:last-of-type::after`, in the footer's colour). The menu floats over the footer's padding, never over its links.
- The extra `pb-16` and techhub's own `pb-24` are removed.

**Search placeholder**
- «ابحث عن منتجات وعلامات تجارية والمزيد...» → «ابحث عن منتج».
- English: "Search for a product".
