# [10-36] Guided setup tip next to the current step

[Back to task list](./tasks.md)

## Description

The guided setup (10-28) showed a bar with the steps, but nothing pointed at where to tap. A small bubble ("Step 2 of 4 · Add your logo and cover photo") now points at the most specific place on screen for the current step: the step's own field on its page (for the logo step, the first of logo, short line and cover still missing; on the policies page the questions, then Save), else the My Store card or checklist button that opens it, else the menu item (sidebar row on computers, the + or More button on phones).

Made to help without getting in the way: one bubble and no backdrop, so nothing is blocked; it follows the target while scrolling, flips to the side with room, and steps aside while a dialog is open or the phone keyboard is up; a target scrolled out of view gets a small pill that scrolls to it; "Got it", the × or Esc fold it to a pulsing dot on the target (tap to open again), remembered for that step only; finishing a step shows "Done! Next: …". It disappears once the essentials are done.

Also: the rows of the homepage editor now show an "Edit" chip, and the top strip row shows its current text (or a prompt to write one), so it is clear they open for editing.
