## Description

Store the brand kit — the facts a merchant gives us once about their business — on the tenant, independent of the active theme, so every theme can use them and switching theme keeps them.

## Requirements

1. New `tenant.settings.brand` with `tagline: { ar, en }`, `coverImage` (URL), `color` ("#rrggbb"), `whatsapp` (E.164), `city: { ar, en }`, `hours: { ar, en }`.
2. Additive only: `storeName`, `storeDescription`, `logo`, `contact` and `socialLinks` stay as they are; nothing renamed or removed.
3. No defaults — an absent value stays absent so themes can tell "not set" from "set to empty". No migration is needed.
4. Arabic is the primary language; English is always optional.
5. Limits are named constants: tagline 140, city 80, hours 200 characters per language; image URL 2048.

## Implementation Plan

- `schemas/tenant.js`: `settings.brand` sub-document, no defaults, comment pointing at the single write path.
- `utils/brandKit.js` (new, pure): `BRAND_TEXT_MAX_LENGTH`, `normalizeBrandColor`, `normalizeBrandImageUrl` (https or `/uploads/...` only), `normalizeBilingualText`, `unwrapWhatsappLink`, `publicBrand`.
- `utils/storeContact.js` (new): the contact-field rules lifted out of `controllers/settings.js` so the bulk settings PUT and the profile API share them (behaviour unchanged).
