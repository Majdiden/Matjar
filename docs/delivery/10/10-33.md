# [10-33] Theme switch carries the homepage words and photo

[Back to task list](./tasks.md)

## Description

Switching to a theme used for the first time showed the subtitle (bound to the tagline) but lost the title, button text, photo and top strip: each theme names its hero settings differently. `services/themeSwitchCarry.js` maps them by role (title, subtitle, button, photo) to the keys the new theme's hero declares, with their Arabic twins, and carries the top strip. Stock photos are not carried; a theme the merchant used before still comes back as they left it; colours and custom CSS still stay with their theme.
