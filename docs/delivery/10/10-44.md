# [10-44] Homepage of a store with no products: the hero, then "No products to show yet"

[Back to task list](./tasks.md)

## Description

A store with no products showed empty product sections on its homepage. Now, on every theme, the homepage keeps only its hero and shows one message in their place: "No products to show yet" / «لا توجد منتجات لعرضها بعد».

- `_shared/theme/homeSections.ts` (pure): `gateHomeSections(sections, hasProducts)` keeps the hero sections (any type naming a hero, as in `services/themeSwitchCarry.js`) and asks for the message when `hasProducts` is false. While unknown (null) everything renders as before, so a store with products never loses its sections.
- `_shared/hooks/useStoreHasProducts.ts`: one `GET /storefront/products?limit=1` per page load, shared by every caller; a failed request counts as unknown.
- `_shared/components/sections/NoProductsYet.tsx`: the message, in each theme's colours and fonts.
- Used by the shared `SectionRenderer` (homepage only) and by the 9 themes that render their own homepage loop.
