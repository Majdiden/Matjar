# [10-63] Delivery zone prices in the zone's currency

[Back to task list](./tasks.md)

## Description

A delivery zone's price was always read in the pricing currency. A merchant pricing in USD who typed the Saudi delivery price as 30 (meaning SAR) charged 30 USD, which became 112.50 SAR.

**What changes**
- **Schema:** zones have `currency` (null = the pricing currency).
- **Dashboard zone form:**
  - "أسعار التوصيل بـ" (delivery prices in) lists the pricing currency and the currencies with an exchange rate.
  - It defaults to the currency of the market covering the zone's first country.
  - Price labels show the currency ("Price (SAR)").
- **Zone list:** shows each price with its currency, instead of a hard-coded "$".
- **Server:**
  - `calculateShipping` converts the zone price to the pricing currency with the store's rate, unrounded, so the customer pays exactly the written amount (30 SAR).
  - Saving a zone in a currency without a rate is refused (400, `ZONE_CURRENCY_NO_RATE`).
- **Delivery policy page:** each zone's price is shown in its own currency.

Existing zones keep the pricing currency until the merchant picks another.
